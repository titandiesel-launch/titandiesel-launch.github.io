The City’s Fresh Breath

A launch-night proposal for the Titan Diesel hybrid tractor. Diesel on the road, electric in the city.

Outside the city, the tractor crosses the desert with a load, on diesel. Inside the city, the same tractor runs on electric power with the diesel engine off. On launch night that truth happens in front of the guests. The reveal itself is the proof.

One night in four parts

The guests begin the night in a city they know every day: grey, crowded and smoggy — but alive. In a round amphitheatre a truck crosses the desert on a curved LED wall, the wall slides open and Titan Diesel’s hybrid tractor rolls out in silence on its own electric power; the desert becomes a blue holographic city. A separate route takes guests to dinner in the forest, and they leave along a street that comes back to life under their hands.

The Grey City

After check-in, guests walk through a narrow corridor into a city built inside the hall: grey buildings, tangled wires, coloured neon and a little smog. The ground floors are shops that serve them; a newspaper seller, a speaker and a street musician perform.
