01 · The idea

A tractor that drives in
on its own electric power.

Outside the city, the tractor climbs mountain roads with a load on diesel. Inside the city, the same tractor runs on electric power with the diesel engine off. On launch night that truth happens in front of the guests: the tractor drives out of the mountain into the city, silently, on its own electric power. So the reveal is itself the proof — not a claim on a screen. The whole night is built on that truth.

Titan Diesel brings a hybrid tractor to Iran’s roads: diesel for the long haul, electric for the city. On launch night the proof is not a claim — it is the tractor itself, driving in silence.

The proposed name

The City’sFreshBreath

One journey, two powers — and the city takes a fresh breath.

It is exactly what the guests see happen: the grey city turns green as the electric tractor arrives. The name is simple, speaks everyday language and makes no exaggerated technical claim; ‘one journey, two powers’ adds a simple, accurate explanation of the hybrid technology.

The concept

One night,
three parts.

The guests begin the night in a city they know every day: grey, crowded and smoggy — but alive. In the runway hall a holographic city builds itself on the LED wall, and Titan Diesel’s hybrid tractor rolls silently out of the mountain on electric power. The city turns green and a herd of mammoths sets off. A separate route takes guests to dinner in the forest, and they leave through a second city — one that has come back to life.

01
Part 1

The Grey City

After check-in, guests walk through a narrow corridor into a city built inside the hall: grey buildings, tangled wires, coloured neon and a little smog. The ground floors are shops that serve them; a newspaper seller, a speaker and a street musician perform.

02
Part 2

The Hybrid Pulse

A holographic city builds itself on the LED wall. The tunnel fills with light, a truck made of light and the name GREAT WALL form on the wall, and the real tractor rolls out silently on electric power. The city turns green, mammoths run, and a brass band and singer perform in front of the tractor.

03
Part 3

Project Oxygen

Guests take a separate route to dinner, without going back into the grey street: a hall with the grandeur of a forest — a forest on LED, real trees and tables dressed with forest-style arrangements. After dinner they leave through a second city: a smaller hall like the first, but full of flowers, vines and trees in warm light.

Design language and colour

Four worlds,
one story.

Design language: the street before the reveal — grey, soot, cold sodium light, light haze and pink and cyan neon; the runway hall — a holographic cyan-white city on deep navy that turns green and bright when the tractor exits; the exit city — green, warm and full of flowers; dinner — forest green, pink and white flowers and warm gold. Materials: painted particleboard and MDF, the folded panels of the origami mountain, an asphalt-look floor, LED, real trees, flowers and plants.

Venue and safety

The hall needs a high ceiling, a truck-sized door and a strong floor; heights and loads are confirmed after the site visit. All timber, fabric and branches are fire-treated, and haze is used only in coordination with the fire-alarm system. The tractor is never seen in smog; the pollution message stays positive and solution-led.
