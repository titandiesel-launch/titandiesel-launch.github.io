Before we build: the truck, the venue, the content and the people — and what we need, from whom.

Before we build

The truck

  • A hybrid may auto-start its diesel when charge drops or a system draws power. The factory must provide an approved 'diesel lockout' — including any fuel-fired heater.
  • A sudden air-brake purge exactly at the silent moment; test it on this truck.
  • Brakes, steering and cooling must work fully without the diesel.
  • Do both sources drive together? No combined torque curve until engineering confirms.

The venue

  • Once the venue is chosen, the whole tractor route signed off by a structural engineer: axle loads, width with mirrors, tallest point, ramps, trench covers.
  • Three separate permits: self-powered move-in, movement with guests present, static display.
  • Haze only with the venue's and fire service's approval; a lit one-way lane, barriers and a marshal who can hold the cue.
  • Get fuel-tank and battery rules in writing — figures like 'one-eighth tank' are rules elsewhere; every venue has its own.

Protocol

  • Officials are not placed in the moving truck, and the start of the reveal does not depend on any action of theirs.
  • The reveal comes before the speeches so every VIP is present for the key moment; a 'hold' cue covers any delay.
  • No full blackout or siren sounds; safe light stays on the guest area (level set by the safety team).
  • The urban narrative stays focused on the tractor's performance; the tone is pride and relief, not criticism of the status quo.
  • A government visit is not a government endorsement; each office's protocol and security approve titles and photos.

Messages and claims

  • The phrase 'double the power' appears in no script until its exact definition and test are confirmed: double what, versus which model.
  • 'Zero tailpipe emissions in electric mode' instead of 'zero pollution'.
  • No promise of exemption from pollution-day truck bans; exemptions depend on cargo type, not on being hybrid.
  • Using less diesel doesn't automatically mean more quota; a calculator must separate quota fuel, market fuel, electricity and battery.
  • Commercial partners are named only with Titan Diesel's approval.

What we need, from whom.

From Titan Diesel

  • Spec sheet: axle loads, size with mirrors, operating modes
  • Exact definition of 'double power' and 'less fuel'
  • Approved diesel lockout and air-brake behaviour
  • One or two units? External charging?
  • One real fleet customer for the night
  • Loaded road-test film with verified weight and grade

From the venue

  • Confirmation of the venue and its exact plan
  • Door and route dimensions, floor load limits
  • Three permits: move-in, movement with guests, static display
  • Rules on fuel, battery, haze and fire alarms
  • Build and rehearsal days

From our team

  • Truck route drawing from outside to the stop mark
  • Design of the 'The City's Fresh Breath' identity and invitation; a 3D model of the hall, city and mountains for mapping design
  • Space design: foyer, stage wall, lounge
  • Test the press-photo angle
  • Full rehearsal plan with the real truck

Suggested timeline: week 1 venue selection, site visit and permits · weeks 2–3 road-test filming · week 4 design and build · two days before: full rehearsal with the truck.
