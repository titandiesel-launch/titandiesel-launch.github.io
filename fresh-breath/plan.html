The night The plan

Four hours, five minutes that matter

200 guests, about 1,500 m², one hall. Run of show, the spaces, how every part is built, and what must be confirmed before we start.

Run of show

  1. 18:30

    Check-in

    Counter in the foyer; the hall stays hidden behind the wall.

  2. 18:40

    The tunnel

    Welcome drinks and standing reception between the grey-city walls; touch walls, photo booth and photo stand open.

  3. 19:40

    Into the hall

    Guests take their seats at the round tables; protocol after the main guest is seated.

  4. 20:00

    The grey city

    The hall dims; the curved LED wall shows the smoggy city.

  5. 20:03

    The wall opens

    The gate slides apart; the tractor rolls out on electric, in silence, onto the stage.

  6. 20:05

    The city breathes

    The gate closes; the city on the wall turns clean and green.

  7. 20:10

    Speeches

    Presenter at the lectern on stage; CEO, engineering, official guest; official photo with the tractor.

  8. 20:40

    Dinner

    The buffet opens next to the hall; guests eat at their own tables, the tractor lit on stage.

  9. 22:00

    The way out

    Through the same tunnel, now a clean street; photos, and a potted plant for every guest at the counter. Close at 22:30.

Golden rule. The reveal happens only after the main guest is seated and before any speech. The gate opens on the stage manager’s call. If the tractor cannot move with guests present, it is parked behind the gate in advance and the wall opens on it standing in light — the movement is never replaced by animation.

The spaces

Guest route

  • Foyer and check-in counter, outside the hall.
  • Two doorways into the tunnel, one at each end of the counter wall.
  • From the tunnel into the main hall; the buffet opens to the hall.
  • Back through the same tunnel, now clean, to the counter for the gift.

Areas

  • Check-in foyer — about 210 m²
  • Tunnel — about 240 m², 8 m wide
  • Main hall with stage — about 670 m², 24 round tables of 8–9
  • Behind the LED wall — about 170 m², truck waiting area and loading door
  • Buffet — about 110 m²
  • Kitchen and service — about 100 m²

Practical notes

  • The tractor enters through the loading door and waits behind the gate; its route never crosses the guests’.
  • Ceiling height, floor load, loading door and power are confirmed at the site visit.
  • Emergency exits stay clear in every space.

How we build it

The tunnel

  • Two interactive LED walls, about 24 m long and 4 m high, facing each other.
  • Footage: a real residential street, grey and smoggy on arrival, clean and green on the way out.
  • Touch: sensors at hand height and a real-time engine; every effect stays on the screen surface.
  • Standing tables with white linen; welcome drinks and finger food served by waiters.

The photo set and stand

  • A curved 3D set about 6 m wide: a built grey facade turning into a living plant wall with flower boxes.
  • The pulse line and leaf in cyan-to-green neon across the set; half concrete, half grass floor.
  • A professional photographer with studio light.
  • The Fresh Breath photo stand: one photo becomes three versions of the city, sent to the guest’s phone by QR code or Bluetooth — no prints.

The hall and the reveal

  • One curved LED wall, about 24 m by 6 m, with a 6 m motorised sliding gate at its centre; the picture runs across it seamlessly when closed.
  • A wide stage, raised about 60 cm, on an engineered steel deck rated for the tractor’s weight.
  • Round tables of 8–9 around the front and both sides of the stage.
  • Moving heads and washes on the truss; haze only behind the gate.

Identity

  • Deep Night surfaces, the pulse-and-leaf mark, cyan-to-green light lines.
  • Identity panels on the check-in wall, the buffet and the photo stand.
  • Titan Diesel | GWM lockup on every main surface.

Dinner

  • Self-service buffet next to the hall: Persian and international dishes, chefs at the counter.
  • Guests eat at their own tables; no separate dining room.
  • Low white and green flowers on the tables, no candles.

Safety

  • Structural sign-off for the stage deck, truss and LED walls.
  • The gate has safety sensors and a manual stop.
  • The tractor moves at walking pace with a guide; a safe distance from the front tables.
  • Haze only in coordination with the fire system; all set materials fire-treated.

Before we build

  • Floor load and loading door size
  • Power capacity and rigging points

From Titan Diesel

  • Guest list and protocol order
  • Approval of the identity and invitation

From us

  • LED and touch-wall content
  • Full rehearsal with the real tractor the day before
