Design notes

How Project Oxygen is built

A separate route to dinner

Speeches on the stage and the official photo beside the tractor. Guests then leave the runway hall through two doors on its west side and go straight to dinner — they do not go back into the grey street. The dinner hall has the grandeur of a forest: a forest on LED, real trees and a ceiling of branches with tiny lights. On the tables, low forest-style arrangements: moss, stones, ferns and white wild flowers.

Out through the second city

After dinner, guests leave through the second city: a smaller hall built like the first, but full of flowers, vines on the balconies, trees in planters and warm string lights from the start — the city after the fresh breath. It is a separate hall, so nothing in the first street has to change during the night. The parting gift: a small potted plant — a sign of the fresh breath.
